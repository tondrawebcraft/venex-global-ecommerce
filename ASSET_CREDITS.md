# Image and brand assets

The small set of storefront photos under `frontend/public/images/` are illustrative stock images downloaded from Unsplash and bundled locally so the storefront does not depend on a third-party image host at runtime. The active demo catalog uses `hero-v2.jpg`, `weekender.jpg`, `headphones-alt.jpg`, `care-alt.jpg` and `bottle.jpg`.

The original photographer page URLs were not preserved with the downloaded files. Recover and record the exact Unsplash source pages and photographer credits, or replace these photos with VENEX-owned product photography before public commercial use. These pictures are not representations of actual VENEX inventory.

The VENEX wordmark is a small code-rendered vector mark in `frontend/src/components/Brand.jsx`; it is not a third-party logo. No generated bitmap asset is required by the current UI.
